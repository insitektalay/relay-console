- Persons: people/contact records linked to at most one organization and related deals, leads, activities, notes, and email history.
- Organizations: companies/accounts with persons, deals, leads, activities, notes, files, fields, and relationships.
- Deals: sales opportunities tracked through pipelines and stages; include value, status, close date, owner, person/org links, products, notes, activities, and files.
- Leads: prequalified potential deals in the Leads Inbox; can have contacts, labels, activities, notes, and sources.
- Activities: calls, meetings, tasks, and custom activity types tied to person, org, deal, or lead.
- Notes/products/users/filters/fields: sales context, catalog links, ownership, saved views, and custom schema.
- Webhooks: company/application events for added, updated, deleted, and matched-filter object changes.
