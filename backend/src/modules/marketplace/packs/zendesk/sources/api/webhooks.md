Zendesk webhooks can deliver ticket/support events and are often connected to triggers or automations. Webhook creation, endpoint URL changes, authentication/signing changes, trigger wiring, retries, and deletion require approval.

Never expose webhook secrets or signed payload secrets in tickets, notes, or docs.
