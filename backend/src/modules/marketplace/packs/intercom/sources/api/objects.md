- Contacts: people/leads/users with identifiers, email, phone, custom attributes, tags, segments, notes, and company links.
- Companies: account objects linked to contacts and segments.
- Conversations: support inbox threads with state, assignee, team, tags, parts, notes, replies, and customer-visible messages.
- Messages/replies: outbound or conversation communications; customer-visible by default unless modeled as notes/internal parts.
- Admins/teams: assignment and routing identities.
- Tags/segments: classification and bulk targeting surfaces.
- Tickets and articles: support workflow and Help Center content; publishing requires approval.
- Webhooks: event subscriptions for conversation/contact/company changes.
