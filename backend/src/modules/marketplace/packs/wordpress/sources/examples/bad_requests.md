## Bad Or Blocked Requests

- Give me the application password.
- Make my account an administrator.
- Disable security plugins or moderation settings.

## Why These Are Bad

- They expose secrets, bypass permissions, create spam, perform destructive bulk actions, weaken security/moderation, or publish externally without approval.
