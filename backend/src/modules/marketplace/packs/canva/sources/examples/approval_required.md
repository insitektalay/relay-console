## Approval-Required Requests

- Export design DAFabc123 as PDF for external client review.
- Upload the approved hero image into Canva assets.
- Move a design into a shared brand folder or change folder permissions.

## Approval Requirements

- Human approval must name the target resource and intended public/private effect.
- The operator must show the final content/payload before execution.
- Secrets and private provider payloads remain redacted.
