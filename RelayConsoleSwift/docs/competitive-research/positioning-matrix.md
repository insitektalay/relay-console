## Current positioning takeaway

Relay Console should not be positioned as another generic team chat app, command palette, no-code automation product, cloud workspace, general AI assistant, hosted executive assistant/action agent, app builder, coding IDE, issue tracker, enterprise search product, or standalone local coding/computer-use agent. Local code points to a macOS operations console for agent runtimes, marketplace/provider action tooling, approvals, artifacts, cron, and evidence. Raycast is the strongest desktop-command-palette analogue, Zapier is the strongest app-integration/automation analogue, Notion is the strongest workspace/system-of-record analogue, ChatGPT and Claude are the strongest general assistant/computer-use analogues, Lindy and Manus are the strongest hosted assistant/action-agent analogues, Replit Agent is the strongest AI app-building analogue, Cursor/Devin/Open Interpreter are the strongest coding-agent/local-runtime analogues, Linear is the strongest product-development workflow/agent-delegation analogue, Dust/Glean are strong enterprise AI-agent/workspace governance analogues, and Hermes remains the most direct runtime comparable/integration.
